# AI operating model

## Decision rights

The business sponsor owns outcomes and funding. The product owner owns backlog and operational adoption. Engineering owns service reliability. Policy owners approve retrieval content. Security, privacy and consumer-duty owners set mandatory controls. The AI design authority accepts residual technical risk. Finance validates benefits. No single role may both alter a material-action policy and approve the resulting release.

## Lifecycle

Discover the problem; classify the use case; design controls; build the golden set; prove in shadow mode; pass release gates; pilot by cohort; monitor outcomes; review monthly; retire models, prompts and documents deliberately.

## Release evidence

Every release records code commit, model/provider version, prompt and policy bundle versions, golden-set fingerprint, evaluation results, open risks, approvals, rollback target and named service owner. A material change to model, retrieval, tool authority or policy bundle requires re-evaluation.

## Governance cadence

- Daily during pilot: incidents, unsafe attempts, cohort outcomes and adoption friction.
- Weekly: benefit trajectory, overrides, false escalations, retrieval gaps and backlog.
- Monthly: model and policy drift, access review, cost, residual risk and supplier changes.
- Quarterly: strategic portfolio, technical debt, operating-model maturity and retirement decisions.
