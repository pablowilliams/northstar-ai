# ADR 002: Synthetic, reproducible portfolio evidence

Status: accepted.

Decision: ship a fixed-seed synthetic case generator, case-level evidence and independent validator rather than imply access to a real retailer dataset.

Rationale: the portfolio must be inspectable, reproducible and honest. Synthetic evidence can demonstrate experimental design, segmentation and claim discipline without exposing customer data.

Consequence: results justify only a pilot hypothesis. Every CV and product surface must preserve the words “synthetic” and “simulated.”
