# Executive discovery workshop pack

## Purpose

Reach a fact-based decision on which customer-operations problem merits a controlled AI proof, what success means and which actions must remain human.

## Participants

Sponsor, customer operations, front-line colleague, engineering, enterprise architecture, information security, data protection, consumer duty, finance and service design. A decision maker and a control owner must attend; otherwise the workshop captures evidence but does not approve scope.

## Pre-work

- Four weeks of case volumes, handle time, transfers, reopens and complaints by category
- Current policies with owners and effective dates
- System landscape and integration constraints
- Three anonymised difficult cases per participant
- Cost baseline agreed with finance
- Existing risk assessments and vulnerable-customer standard

## 120-minute agenda

1. 0–10: mandate, decision and non-negotiables.
2. 10–30: map the current journey and evidence pain, avoiding solution language.
3. 30–50: identify outcome moments, affected cohorts and failure consequences.
4. 50–70: generate use cases and define the smallest useful unit of assistance.
5. 70–90: score value, feasibility, evidence and controllable risk independently.
6. 90–105: challenge the top two with pre-mortem and red-team prompts.
7. 105–115: agree pilot success, guardrails, stop rules and benefit owner.
8. 115–120: confirm decision log, actions and unanswered evidence.

## Facilitation questions

- Which decision consumes the most skilled time, and why?
- What evidence would change the decision?
- Which failure is merely inconvenient, and which creates customer harm?
- Where does a colleague exercise discretion today?
- What must the system never do even if the customer asks?
- Who can switch the capability off, and what signal would trigger them?
- Which benefit can finance recognise, rather than merely admire?

## Outputs

Signed problem statement, current-state service blueprint, opportunity scores with dissent recorded, data/evidence inventory, initial harm map, pilot cohort, metric dictionary, control boundaries, named owners, decision log and 12-week mobilisation plan.
